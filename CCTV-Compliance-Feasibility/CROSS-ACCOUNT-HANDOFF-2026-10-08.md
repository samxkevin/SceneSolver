It may review bounded evidence, assist with difficult visual predicates, or draft grounded narratives. It does not become the authoritative policy engine.\n\nTrusted sensors are a parallel arm where available. A sensor may replace or corroborate a visual predicate.\n\n## What P0 already proved\nP0 was model neutral and used structured synthetic observations. It passed 11 of 11 rule fixtures, rejected 2 of 2 malformed input fixtures, passed deterministic replay checks, processed 13 structured frames with 7 sampled and 6 skipped, had 0 dropped frames, produced 7 observations, 3 events and 1 track, confirmed a 3 second zone dwell rule, and retained the dwell as open through stream end rather than inventing an exit.\n\nP0 proves that the event, temporal, rule and evidence seam can be exercised independently of a learned model.\n\nP0 does NOT prove detector accuracy, real CCTV FPS, target hardware capacity, quantisation benefit, real world alert quality, VLM value, sensor value, legal approval or production readiness.\n\n## The practical question now\nDo not build the production system. The next practical step is a controlled visual perception feasibility experiment using approved representative or staged CCTV footage while keeping the event and rule contracts unchanged.\n\nFirst rule candidate: person enters restricted zone and remains beyond a deterministic dwell threshold.\n\nThe learned model should expose only the person observation. Tracking, geometry, temporal state, rule evaluation and evidence stay deterministic. This isolates the perception question.\n\n## Practical sequence\nP1: freeze a small approved video corpus and manifest. Record clip hash, camera, resolution, FPS, codec, duration, scene conditions, privacy class, zone geometry, boxes and entry or dwell ground truth.\n\nP2: run one detector through the existing normalized observation seam. Do not rewrite the rule engine. Do not add the VLM. Do not rebuild the full SceneSolver AE, TimeSformer, RL and LLaVA stack.\n\nP3: run the same clips through Arena and measure observation precision and recall, small object recall, missed entries, false zone entries, dwell error, false alerts per camera hour, unknown intervals, p50 and p95 latency, model load time, RAM or VRAM, processed FPS, skipped frames and dropped frames.\n\nP4: add one challenger only after the first candidate is measured. Current frontier queue: DINOv3, SAM3 or SAM3.1, Grounding DINO 1.6 and DINO X. The choice must follow task fit and incremental measurement value, not novelty.\n\nP5: use ablation to justify every extra component. Segmentation, pose, temporal action recognition, VLM review and sensors are retained only when they provide measurable value that survives resource, privacy, reliability and licensing gates.\n\n## How SceneSolver is treated\nExisting SceneSolver models are preserved.
